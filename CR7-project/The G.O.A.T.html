About The Legend

This page is dedicated to the GOAT, Cristiano Ronaldo.

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward and captains both the Portugal national team and Saudi Pro League club Al Nassr.

"Your love makes me strong, your hate makes me unstoppable." - CR7

Known for his incredible work ethic and athleticism.
He has won 5 Ballon d'Or awards and 4 European Golden Shoes.
